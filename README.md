## What is this sorcery?

D-SARAL is yet another revolutionary data cleaning tool that promises to solve all your messy data problems with the power of... wait for it... systematic approaches and documented decisions! Revolutionary stuff, really. I'm sure no one has ever thought of analyzing data quality before.

## Features (because apparently we need to list these)

- 🔍 **Comprehensive Analysis**: Because apparently people can't just look at their data and figure out what's wrong
- 🧹 **Automated Cleaning**: For when you're too busy to manually fix your obvious data issues
- 📊 **Real-time Progress**: Watch your data get cleaner in real-time! (It's almost like magic, but with more waiting)
- 📦 **ZIP Export**: Because who doesn't love compressed files?
- 🎨 **Beautiful UI**: A wizard interface so intuitive, even your grandmother could probably figure it out (assuming she's into data cleaning)

## Usage (for those who like following instructions)

1. Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost:5000`
2. Marvel at our revolutionary 4-step wizard interface
3. Select your file type (CSV, JSON, or TXT - because apparently we need to limit choices)
4. Upload your files (this is where the magic happens)
5. Watch as our sophisticated algorithms identify issues that you could probably spot with basic data literacy
6. Download your "cleaned" data and pretend you learned something

## Deployment (because local development is for amateurs)

### Render Deployment (our preferred method of overcomplicating things):

1. Push your code to GitHub (because apparently we can't just share folders anymore)
2. Create a Render account (more accounts? Sure, why not?)
3. Connect your repository (follow the prompts like a good developer)
4. Set your start command to `gunicorn app_fixed:app` (memorize this for job interviews)
5. Deploy and watch your free tier spin down every 15 minutes (efficiency!)

### PythonAnywhere Alternative (for the nostalgically inclined):

1. Create an account on PythonAnywhere (because free hosting is apparently a premium feature now)
